Every Dot Counts Support

Thanks for playing "Every Dot Counts" — the count-the-dots puzzle where the sneakiest dots hide in plain sight. This page answers common questions and tells you how to reach us.

FAQ

The answer seems higher than the dots on the board

Every dot counts — including the ones hiding in the text. The dots on letters like "i" and "j", and in punctuation like ".", "!", "?" and "…", all count toward the answer. Check the phrase at the top and the small tagline at the bottom of the card. The reveal after each guess shows you exactly where every dot was.

Can I continue a run after closing the app?

Yes. Your run in progress is saved automatically on your device. When you come back, tap CONTINUE on the home screen to pick up where you left off. A run ends for good when you run out of lives.

I lost my progress after reinstalling

Records and settings are stored on your device only. Deleting the App deletes them; there is currently no cloud backup or transfer between devices.

About ads

The App is free to play thanks to ads. Rewarded ads are always your choice — watch one only when you want a hint or to continue a run. You can change ad-personalization permissions anytime in your device settings. The game is fully playable if you decline.
